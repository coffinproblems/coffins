Let $ABC$ be a triangle.
Let $\gamma$ be its incircle.  % in \cite[problem~24]{khovanova2005coffinsmain} it is mistranslated as circumscribed circle
Let $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$ be circles such that
$\alpha_1$ is tangent to $\overline{BC}$, to $\overline{CA}$, and to $\gamma$ externally;
$\alpha_2$ is tangent to $\overline{AB}$, to $\overline{CA}$, and to $\gamma$ externally;
$\alpha_3$ is tangent to $\overline{AB}$, to $\overline{BC}$, and to $\gamma$ externally.
Determine the radius of $\gamma$, given the radii of $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$.

\medskip
Alernative formulation.

Three circles are each tangent to a (distinct) unordered pair of (distinct) sides of a triangle
and to the incircle of the triangle, externally.
Determine the radius of the incircle, given the radii of the three circles.
